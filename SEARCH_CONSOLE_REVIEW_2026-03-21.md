# Search Console Review: `agadirlocalguide.com`

Reporting window: December 20, 2025 to March 19, 2026  
Sources reviewed: `Chart.csv`, `Queries.csv`, `Pages.csv`, `Countries.csv`, `Devices.csv`, `Search appearance.csv`, `Filters.csv`, plus current page metadata/canonical tags in the local repo.

## Executive Summary

`agadirlocalguide.com` is gaining visibility, but that growth is not yet converting into clicks efficiently. Over the 90-day window the site generated `160` clicks from `9,226` impressions for an overall CTR of `1.73%`, with a weighted average position of about `12.89`.

The main pattern is a widening gap between impressions and clicks. Monthly impressions rose from `497` in December 2025 to `3,076` in March 2026, while CTR fell from `3.42%` to `0.98%`. That usually means Google is testing the site on more non-brand queries, but the current pages are either not ranking high enough yet, not matching search intent closely enough, or not earning the click even when they do appear on page 1.

Brand is carrying the account. Non-brand discovery exists across Paradise Valley, quad biking, day trips, family travel, cruise, and Souss Massa topics, but those clusters produce very few clicks relative to their impression volume. This is the core SEO opportunity.

## Key Findings

### 1. Brand traffic is doing the heavy lifting

Brand-like queries generated almost all meaningful clicks:

- `agadir local guide`: `42` clicks, `211` impressions, `19.91%` CTR, position `1.27`
- `the local guide`: `3` clicks, `5` impressions, `60%` CTR, position `1`
- `local guide`: `2` clicks, `21` impressions, `9.52%` CTR, position `4.24`

By contrast, only `7` of `316` exported queries produced any clicks at all.

Implication:

- The site has clear brand relevance.
- It has not yet converted that into dependable non-brand acquisition.

## Page-Level Opportunities

### Homepage: `https://agadirlocalguide.com/`

- Performance: `103` clicks, `1,283` impressions, `8.03%` CTR, position `5.9`
- Current title: `Agadir Tours & Excursions – Local Expert Guides | From €25`
- Current description emphasizes tours, excursions, pickup, and prices.

Assessment:

- This is the site’s strongest asset and probably captures most brand demand.
- It also appears for generic terms like `agadir`, `guide agadir`, and `agadir 2026`, which suggests Google sees it as a broad entry point.

Opportunity:

- Preserve this page as the brand/commercial hub.
- Strengthen internal links from the homepage to your highest-impression blog and tour pages using exact-intent anchors such as “things to do in Agadir,” “Paradise Valley guide,” “quad biking in Agadir,” and “day trips from Agadir.”
- Do not overload the homepage with every query variation; use it to distribute authority.

### `/blog/top-10-things-to-do-agadir-2026.html`

- Performance: `8` clicks, `1,234` impressions, `0.65%` CTR, position `6.44`
- Current title: `Top 10 Things to Do in Agadir 2026 | Ultimate Local Guide`
- Current description is broad and list-style.

Assessment:

- This is the clearest underperforming winner. A page at position `6.44` with `1,234` impressions should be generating materially more than `8` clicks.
- Query evidence includes `things to do in agadir`, `what to do in agadir`, and family-related variations.

Likely causes:

- Title is generic and does not differentiate enough in the SERP.
- Snippet may read like a general tourism article rather than the most useful planning result.
- Intent may be split with the family-specific article and the travel guide page.

Recommendation:

- Rework title/description around searcher intent, not branding. Focus on “best things to do in Agadir,” “2026,” and stronger benefit language.
- Add clearer sections for families, couples, cruise visitors, and first-time visitors so more long-tail variants can map to one strong URL.
- Add stronger internal links from this page into related commercial pages.

### `/blog/paradise-valley-agadir-guide.html`

- Current title: `Paradise Valley Agadir 2026 | Complete Local Guide`

## Structural SEO Findings

### 1. Duplicate URL families are visible in Search Console

Confirmed families include:

- `/tours/tour-souss-massa-park-agadir.html`
- `/tour-souss-massa-park-agadir.html`
- `https://www.agadirlocalguide.com/tour-souss-massa-park-agadir.html`

- `/day-trips-from-agadir.html`
- `https://www.agadirlocalguide.com/day-trips-from-agadir.html`

- `/tour-paradise-valley-agadir.html`
- `https://www.agadirlocalguide.com/tour-paradise-valley-agadir.html`

- `/tour-quad-biking-agadir.html`
- `https://www.agadirlocalguide.com/tour-quad-biking-agadir.html`

Impact:

- Google is still seeing or retaining legacy/variant URLs.
- That can split signals, muddle canonical choice, and slow ranking consolidation.

### 2. Legacy day-trip pages correctly canonicalize to new URLs, but the migration is still incomplete in search

Verified in source:

- `/tours/day-trip-taroudant-from-agadir.html` canonicalizes to `/tours/day-trip-taroudant-from-agadir-new.html`
- `/tours/day-trip-marrakech-from-agadir.html` canonicalizes to `/tours/day-trip-marrakech-from-agadir-new.html`

Impact:

- Canonicals are directionally correct.
- But if legacy URLs still resolve with `200 OK` instead of redirecting, Google can continue surfacing them as separate page entities.

### 3. Image URLs are appearing in the page report

Examples:

- `/img/excursions/Agadir City Tour/agadir 1.jpg`
- `/img/blog/paradise-valley-blog.webp`
- `/img/excursions/Paradise Valley/paradise 1.jpeg`

Impact:

- Not a primary growth issue, but it suggests some image assets are indexable as standalone results and are entering the Search Console page report.
- That adds noise to page-level reporting.

### 4. Template comments are present at the top of some tour pages

Observed in:

- `/tours/tour-souss-massa-park-agadir.html`
- `/tours/tour-agadir-guided-city-tour-cable-car.html`

Impact:

- This does not directly hurt rankings by itself.
- It is a signal that some tour pages are still close to template form, which often correlates with weaker differentiation across similar pages.

## Prioritized Action Plan

### Quick wins: next 1-2 weeks

1. Consolidate duplicate URL families with redirects, not just canonicals.
2. Audit `www` vs non-`www` behavior and enforce one host consistently.
3. Rework titles and descriptions on the highest-impression low-CTR pages:
   - `/blog/top-10-things-to-do-agadir-2026.html`
   - `/blog/paradise-valley-agadir-guide.html`
   - `/blog/things-to-do-agadir-families.html`
   - `/blog/quad-biking-agadir-guide.html`
   - `/blog/agadir-cruise-excursions.html`
   - `/tours/tour-souss-massa-park-agadir.html`
   - `/tours/tour-agadir-guided-city-tour-cable-car.html`
4. Add stronger internal links from the homepage and travel guide hub to the pages above using query-matching anchors.
5. Decide a single primary URL for each commercial topic so blog pages and tour pages are not competing for the same head term without a deliberate intent split.

### Medium-term: next 2-6 weeks

1. Build clearer content architecture by intent:
   - homepage = brand/commercial hub
   - blog guides = informational hub
   - tour pages = transactional pages
2. Expand the Paradise Valley, cruise, family, and quad biking pages around the exact subtopics already appearing in query data.
3. Strengthen day-trip coverage with one clear hub page and cleaner consolidation between old and new URLs.
4. Review whether `all-tours.html` deserves optimization or should remain de-emphasized, since it currently has `274` impressions at position `44.04`.

### Monitoring: next 30-60 days

Track these pages weekly after updates:

- homepage
- top 10 things to do
- Paradise Valley guide
- Souss Massa tour
- family activities page
- quad biking guide
- cruise excursions page
- city tour cable car page

Track these metrics:

- clicks
- impressions
- CTR
- average position
- query mix by page
- duplicate URL impressions disappearing over time

Success signal:

- CTR on high-impression page-1/early-page-2 URLs should improve first.
- Click growth should then follow without needing a proportional increase in total impressions.

## Final Assessment

The site is not in a low-visibility state. It is in a transition state: Google is already testing it across valuable non-brand topics, but the current architecture and page targeting are not yet strong enough to turn that exposure into traffic.

The best near-term gains are not from publishing many new pages. They are from consolidating duplicates, sharpening intent separation between guide and tour URLs, and improving SERP targeting on the handful of pages that already have meaningful impression volume.
