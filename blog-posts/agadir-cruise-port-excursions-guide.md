## Port Logistics: What You Need to Know

### Getting Off the Ship

When your cruise ship docks at Agadir:

1. **Terminal Location:** Ships dock at the commercial port or marina
2. **Port Exit:** Walk through the terminal to the exit area (5-10 minutes)
3. **Meeting Point:** Local guides typically meet you just outside the port gates
4. **Identification:** Look for your guide holding a sign with your name

### If You Book With Us

Here's exactly what happens:

1. **Before Your Cruise:** We confirm your ship name, arrival time, and departure time
2. **Day Before:** We WhatsApp you with meeting point details and guide contact
3. **Port Day:** Your guide waits at the designated meeting point with a sign
4. **During Tour:** We track your ship's schedule and monitor any changes
5. **Return:** We guarantee return 60-90 minutes before departure

### Currency & Money

**Do you need Moroccan Dirhams?**
- Euros are widely accepted in tourist areas
- Credit cards work at larger shops and restaurants
- ATMs available near the port and in city center
- For souks/markets, local currency gets better prices

**Our Recommendation:** Bring €50-100 in cash for shopping/tips. We accept euros or card payment for tours.

### WiFi & Communication

- Port area has limited WiFi
- Buy a local SIM card at the port (cheap data available)
- Most cafes in town have free WiFi
- WhatsApp is the best way to communicate with guides

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is it safe to book a shore excursion outside the cruise line?

Absolutely! Thousands of cruise passengers book independent tours in Agadir every year without issues. The key is choosing a reputable local operator with good reviews, confirmed meeting procedures, and guaranteed return times. We've been welcoming cruise passengers for 10+ years with a 100% return rate.

### How do I know I'll get back to the ship on time?

We guarantee return 60-90 minutes before your ship's departure. We know Agadir traffic patterns, we track ship schedules, and we've never had a passenger miss their ship. Your guide will have the ship's departure time confirmed and will manage the itinerary accordingly.

### What if my cruise ship changes its schedule?

We monitor schedule changes and adjust accordingly. If your port time is significantly reduced, we'll offer a modified tour or refund. Your guide will have our contact number for real-time updates.

### How much money should I bring for a shore excursion?

For a guided tour: €40-60 per person (tour cost) + €30-50 for shopping/extras. We accept euros and credit cards. For independent exploring, bring €50-100 in cash plus card backup.

### What should I wear for a shore excursion in Agadir?

Agadir is relaxed—casual, comfortable clothing is fine. For Paradise Valley, bring swimwear and water shoes. For city tours, comfortable walking shoes. Morocco is Muslim but Agadir is tourist-friendly; modest clothing is appreciated but not strictly required.

### Can I do a shore excursion on my own without a guide?

Yes, but we recommend a guide for first-time visitors. Taxis are available at the port, but you'll need to negotiate prices, navigate language barriers, and manage your own timing. A guide provides local knowledge, handles logistics, and ensures you're back on time.

### Do you offer private shore excursions?

Yes! Private tours are available for couples, families, or groups. You'll have your own guide and vehicle with a fully customized itinerary. Private tours typically cost €150-250 for 2-4 people (total, not per person).

### What if I have mobility issues?

Most tours can be adapted for limited mobility. Paradise Valley involves some walking on uneven terrain (not suitable for wheelchairs). City tours and beach/camel rides are more accessible. Contact us with your specific needs and we'll advise.

### How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking 1-2 weeks before your cruise. During peak season (December-March), book earlier to ensure availability. We can sometimes accommodate last-minute bookings via WhatsApp.

### What's included in your shore excursion prices?

All our tours include: port pickup and drop-off, air-conditioned transport, English-speaking guide, entrance fees, and usually lunch. Tips are not included but appreciated.
