## Why Quad Biking is the #1 Adventure Activity in Agadir

Agadir has become Morocco's adventure capital, and quad biking sits at the top of every traveler's bucket list. But why is it so popular here compared to other destinations?

**The terrain is unlike anywhere else in Morocco.** Within a single tour, you'll experience golden sand dunes, eucalyptus forests, dried riverbeds, and traditional Berber villages. The landscape changes every few kilometers, keeping the adventure fresh and exciting throughout.

Unlike Marrakech's desert tours that require 3+ hours of driving just to reach the dunes, Agadir's quad biking locations are just 20-30 minutes from your hotel. This means more riding time and less sitting in a vehicle.

The weather also plays a huge role. Agadir enjoys 300+ days of sunshine per year, making quad biking possible year-round. Even in winter, temperatures rarely drop below 18°C during the day—perfect conditions for outdoor adventures.

And here's something most tourists don't realize: the Agadir region has some of the most diverse quad biking terrain in all of Africa. From beach riding along the Atlantic coast to challenging dune climbs in the Saharan outskirts, there's a trail for every skill level.

## What to Wear & Bring for Quad Biking

### What to Wear

**Essentials:**
- **Closed-toe shoes** - Sneakers or hiking boots work best. No sandals or flip-flops.
- **Long pants** - Protects from sun, sand, and minor scrapes. Jeans or hiking pants are ideal.
- **Light layers** - A t-shirt with a light jacket. Desert mornings can be cool.
- **Sunglasses** - We provide goggles, but bring sunglasses for before/after.

**Seasonal considerations:**
- **Summer (June-August):** Light, breathable clothing. It gets hot!
- **Winter (December-February):** Bring a warm jacket for morning tours.
- **Spring/Fall:** Perfect conditions—light layers are enough.

### What to Bring

✅ **Sunscreen** (SPF 50+ recommended)  
✅ **Phone/camera** with protective case or ziplock bag  
✅ **Small amount of cash** (€5-10 for tips, optional)  
✅ **Water bottle** (we provide water, but extra never hurts)  

### What NOT to Bring

❌ Expensive jewelry or watches  
❌ Large bags or backpacks  
❌ Flip-flops or open-toed shoes  
❌ Loose scarves that could get caught  

We have secure storage at our base for valuables you don't want to take on the tour.
