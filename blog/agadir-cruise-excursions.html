Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to book a shore excursion outside the cruise line?

Absolutely! Thousands of cruise passengers book independent tours in Agadir every year. The key is choosing a reputable local operator with good reviews and guaranteed return times.

How do I know I'll get back to the ship on time?

We guarantee return 60-90 minutes before departure. We know the port schedule and traffic patterns intimately. In 10+ years, we've never had a passenger miss their ship.

What if my ship's schedule changes?

We monitor all ship arrivals. If your arrival time changes, we adjust accordingly. If the port call is cancelled, you get a full refund—no questions asked.

How much cheaper are independent tours?

Typically 30-50% cheaper. A tour the ship sells for €80 might cost €40-50 with us—same experience, smaller groups, local expertise.
