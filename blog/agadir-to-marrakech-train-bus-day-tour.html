Agadir to Marrakech Train or Bus? Distance, Taxi, and Day Trip Guide

See the real distance, travel time, transfer trade-offs, and whether a Marrakech day trip from Agadir is easier than changing hotels.

Yassine
Apr 11, 2026 • 11 min read

If you are searching Agadir to Marrakech train, Agadir to Marrakech bus, or Agadir to Marrakech distance, this page gives you the practical answer first, then the best option by travel style. If you are still deciding whether the route is worth doing at all, start with Agadir or Marrakech: which is better for your trip?, then use this guide for the logistics.

Quick answer: best way to go from Agadir to Marrakech

  • Need cheapest seat: Bus (CTM / Supratours style operators).
  • Need fastest door-to-door: Private transfer or rental car.
  • Need one-day visit with no logistics: Guided Marrakech day trip from Agadir.
  • Need train: No direct passenger rail on this route yet.

Is there a train from Agadir to Marrakech?

No direct passenger train currently runs between Agadir and Marrakech. As of April 11, 2026, travelers on this route use road transport. Morocco's rail network serves Marrakech from the north, while Agadir is still outside the operational passenger rail map for this city pair.

This is why many people searching for "train" end up booking coaches. Before paying, check the mode (rail vs coach) on the operator page so you know exactly what vehicle and station you are booking.

Agadir to Marrakech distance and travel time

The route is about 250 km (155 miles) by road. In typical traffic, travel time is usually:

  • Private car / transfer: around 3 to 3.5 hours one way.
  • Intercity bus: around 4 to 5 hours one way.

Journey time changes with season, departure slot, and city traffic on arrival to Marrakech.

Agadir to Marrakech bus (CTM / Supratours and similar)

Bus is the standard budget option and the closest practical substitute for people who expected a train. Typical one-way tickets often sit in the 100 to 180 MAD range, but prices move by demand and schedule.

Pros: lower cost, multiple departures, no driving stress.
Cons: slower total time, fixed timetable, plus local taxi transfer from station to your riad/hotel in Marrakech.

Taxi, private transfer, or rental car

Searches like Agadir to Marrakech taxi price usually come from travelers balancing comfort vs budget. Private transfer is more expensive than bus seats, but gives the easiest door-to-door experience and better control of departure time.

Rental car can work well for confident drivers and multi-stop routes, but for a one-day Marrakech run it can feel tiring once you include city parking, medina walking, and the late return to Agadir.

When a Marrakech day trip from Agadir makes sense

A guided option is often the best value in effort, not just price. With a Marrakech day trip from Agadir, your pickup, route timing, and key stops are pre-planned, so you can focus on the experience instead of intercity logistics.

Expect a long but manageable format: early departure, major highlights in Marrakech, and same-day return, commonly around 12 hours door to door.

Frequently asked questions

Can you do Agadir to Marrakech and back in one day?

Yes. It is a long day, but very common. A guided format is usually easier than DIY if you are not staying overnight in Marrakech.

Is bus or private transfer better for Agadir to Marrakech?

Bus is better for budget. Private transfer is better for comfort, speed, and door-to-door convenience.

Is there a direct train from Agadir to Marrakech in 2026?

No direct passenger rail service is available on this route as of April 11, 2026.

Should I do Marrakech as a day trip or stay overnight?

Choose a day trip if you want highlights without changing hotels. Stay overnight if you want slower medina time, rooftop dinners, and early-morning exploration.

Final recommendation

For most travelers based on the coast, the best balance is simple: keep Agadir as your base, then do Marrakech as one structured day. You avoid hotel changes, cut planning stress, and still get a strong first look at the medina.

If you want deeper Marrakech time (sunrise in the old city, rooftop dinners, late souk walks), stay overnight. If you want convenience and highlights, book the Marrakech day trip from Agadir and keep the rest of your trip relaxed on the coast.
