Agadir to Marrakech: Train, Bus, Distance & Day Trip (2026 Guide)

Agadir to Marrakech is about 250 km by road. There is no direct train, so compare coach, bus, taxi, private transfer, and day-trip options before you choose.

By Yassine·Local guide·Updated May 2026·11 min read

If you are searching Agadir to Marrakech train, Agadir to Marrakech bus, or Agadir to Marrakech distance, the fast answer is this: there is no direct passenger train, the road distance is about 250 km (155 miles), and the practical choices are coach, taxi/private transfer, rental car, or a guided Marrakech day trip from Agadir. If you are still deciding whether the city switch is worth it, start with the Agadir vs Marrakech comparison, then use this page for the logistics.

Route basics: distance, travel time, and train reality

The Agadir to Marrakech route is straightforward, but it is still a proper intercity journey. The road is used every day by coaches, taxis, private transfers, rental cars, and tour vans, so the hard part is not whether you can get there; it is choosing the transport style that fits your time, budget, and comfort level.

The route is about 250 km (155 miles) by road. CTM lists the Marrakech-Agadir coach route at 258 km. In typical traffic, travel time is usually:

  • Private car / transfer: around 3 to 3.5 hours one way.
  • Coach / intercity bus: often around 3.5 to 5 hours, depending on operator, departure, stops, and traffic.

Journey time changes with season, departure slot, road works, and city traffic on arrival to Marrakech.

Train and fast-train options

No direct passenger train currently runs between Agadir and Marrakech. As of May 24, 2026, travelers on this route use road transport. Morocco's rail network serves Marrakech from the north, while Agadir is still outside the operational passenger rail map for this city pair.

That also means there is no fast train or bullet train carrying passengers from Agadir to Marrakech today. Morocco has high-speed rail in the north, and official rail development continues around Marrakech, but for this route you should plan on coach, car, taxi, private transfer, or a guided day trip.

This is why many people searching for "Is there a train between Agadir and Marrakech?" or "Is there a fast train to Marrakech from Agadir?" end up booking coaches. Before paying, check the mode on the operator page so you know whether you are booking rail, coach, or a combined service.

The same route in reverse

If you are wondering "How do I get from Marrakech to Agadir?", use the same route in reverse: coach, private transfer, taxi, rental car, or pre-arranged tour transfer. There is no direct train from Marrakech to Agadir, so do not plan around a rail station arrival in Agadir.

If you are landing in Marrakech and sleeping in Agadir the same night, private transfer is the smoothest option. If you are watching budget, CTM and Supratours-style coaches are the normal public transport choice; confirm the live departure time, luggage rules, and station location before you pay.

Coach, taxi, private transfer, or rental car

For the simplest experience, use a private transfer or guided day trip. For the cheapest seat, use coach. For the most flexible multi-day plan, rent a car only if you are comfortable with motorway driving, Marrakech traffic, and parking near the medina.

Coach and bus from Agadir to Marrakech

Yes. Coach is the main public transport option between the two cities. CTM and Supratours serve the Marrakech-Agadir corridor, and Supratours describes itself as ONCF's coach subsidiary for cities not served by train.

Coach is usually the best DIY choice if you want a lower fare and do not mind fixed departure times. The hidden cost is often time: getting to the station, waiting, then taking a local taxi from the Marrakech station area to your riad, hotel, or meeting point.

Bus and coach prices

Bus and coach fares change by operator, date, seat type, and demand. Recent operator pages show Marrakech-Agadir coach fares starting around 90 to 130 MAD, but treat this as a planning range, not a guaranteed fare.

If your exact question is "How much is the bus from Agadir to Marrakech?", check CTM or Supratours directly for the live schedule, current price, luggage conditions, and exact departure station. This matters most around holidays, weekends, and late-night departures.

Taxi and private-transfer costs

A taxi or private transfer costs much more than a coach seat because you are paying for the vehicle, driver time, fuel, and often a return or waiting arrangement. The final price depends on pickup area, vehicle type, season, number of passengers, and whether you need one-way, return, or full-day waiting.

If you ask "How much is a taxi from Agadir to Marrakech?", the honest answer is that it depends on the full arrangement. Agree the full price before departure and clarify what is included: tolls, pickup, drop-off, waiting time, and late return. If your goal is only to see Marrakech highlights and come back the same day, compare the transfer quote against a guided day trip before you book.

Planning Marrakech as a day trip from Agadir

You can do a day trip from Agadir to Marrakech. It is long, but very common: expect an early start, a full road day, selected Marrakech highlights, and a same-day return to the coast.

A guided option is often the best value in effort, not just price. With a Marrakech day trip from Agadir, your pickup, route timing, and key stops are pre-planned, so you can focus on the experience instead of intercity logistics.

What one day in Marrakech can realistically cover

You can see the Marrakech highlights in one day: medina atmosphere, Jemaa el-Fnaa, selected monuments, souk lanes, and a first taste of the Red City. One day is not enough for slow riad life, late-night wandering, or Atlas side trips, but it works well as a first look from Agadir.

This is usually the best fit for first-time Morocco visitors, resort guests staying in Agadir or Taghazout, and travelers who want Bahia Palace, the medina, and Jemaa el-Fnaa without handling tickets, station transfers, or late-evening road planning themselves.

Bus vs guided day trip

Choose the bus if your priority is the lowest transport cost and you are happy to organize your day independently once you reach Marrakech. Choose the guided day trip if your priority is convenience, hotel pickup, and a smoother first visit where the big logistics decisions are already handled.

For many travelers, the real trade-off is not only price. It is whether you want to spend energy on coach schedules, station taxis, and route planning, or spend that energy inside Marrakech itself.

Frequently asked questions

Is it easy to get from Agadir to Marrakech?

Yes. The road route is straightforward and used daily by coaches, taxis, private transfers, rental cars, and tour vans. The easiest formats are a guided day trip or private transfer; coach is usually the budget option.

How do I get from Marrakech to Agadir?

Use the same route in reverse: coach, private transfer, taxi, rental car, or pre-arranged transfer. There is no direct train from Marrakech to Agadir.

Can you do a day trip from Agadir to Marrakech?

Yes. It is a long day, but very common. A guided format is usually easier than DIY if you are not staying overnight in Marrakech.

Can you see Marrakech in one day?

You can see the main highlights in one day: medina atmosphere, Jemaa el-Fnaa, selected monuments, and souk lanes. Stay overnight if you want slower medina time, rooftop dinners, and early-morning exploration.

No direct passenger rail service is available on this route as of May 24, 2026. Use coach, car, taxi, private transfer, or a guided day trip instead.

Is there a fast train or bullet train to Marrakech from Agadir?

No. Morocco has high-speed rail in the north, but there is no fast train or bullet train from Agadir to Marrakech today.

Is there a coach from Agadir to Marrakech?

Yes. Coach is the main public transport option. CTM and Supratours serve the route, with live schedules and fares changing by date.

How much is the bus from Agadir to Marrakech?

Recent operator pages show Marrakech-Agadir fares starting around 90 to 130 MAD, but check CTM or Supratours directly because prices and seat availability change.

How much is a taxi from Agadir to Marrakech?

Taxi and private-transfer prices vary by vehicle, pickup point, waiting time, and return arrangement. Agree the full price before departure and compare it with a guided day trip if you only want Marrakech highlights.

Final recommendation

For most travelers based on the coast, the best balance is simple: keep Agadir as your base, then do Marrakech as one structured day. You avoid hotel changes, cut planning stress, and still get a strong first look at the medina.

If you want deeper Marrakech time (sunrise in the old city, rooftop dinners, late souk walks), stay overnight. If you want convenience and highlights, book the Marrakech day trip from Agadir and keep the rest of your trip relaxed on the coast.
