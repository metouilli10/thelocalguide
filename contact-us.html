BEFORE YOU GO

Good to know.

A few quick answers before we start planning your day.

What should I include in my message?

Your preferred date, number of travelers, pickup area and the experiences you’re interested in are a great start. You can also tell us if you’d like a private day.

Can you help me choose a tour?

Yes. Tell us what you enjoy and how much time you have. We’ll suggest experiences that fit your plans.

Can I ask about hotel pickup?

Of course. Share your hotel or pickup area and we’ll confirm the pickup options and timing for your chosen tour.

Can we plan a private experience?

Tell us your group size, dates and ideas. We’ll discuss what can be arranged and confirm the details with you.

Can I book through WhatsApp?

Yes. Message us with your preferred tour, date and group size. We’ll check availability and explain the next steps.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellation terms depend on the experience. Please check the details on its tour page, and ask us before booking if you have questions.
