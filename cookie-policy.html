1) Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how Agadir Local Guide ("we", "us", "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on our website.

By using our website, you agree to the use of cookies in accordance with this policy.

2) What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help improve your browsing experience and allow websites to function properly.

3) Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly.

They include:

  • Booking process functionality
  • Security and authentication
  • Session management

These cookies cannot be disabled.

Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors use our website.

They may collect:

  • Pages visited
  • Time spent on pages
  • Traffic sources

We use tools such as Google Analytics to improve our services.

Marketing & Advertising Cookies

These cookies are used to deliver relevant advertisements and track ad performance.

They may be used by platforms like:

  • Google (Google Ads)
  • Meta (Facebook / Instagram Ads)

These cookies allow us to:

  • Show targeted ads
  • Measure campaign performance
  • Retarget visitors who did not complete a booking

Functional Cookies

These cookies enhance your experience by remembering preferences such as:

  • Language
  • Location
  • Previous interactions

4) How You Can Control Cookies

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings.

Common browsers include Chrome, Safari, and Firefox.

Please note that disabling cookies may affect website functionality and your booking experience.

5) Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may be set by third-party services integrated into our website, including:

  • Payment providers such as Stripe
  • Booking systems such as Bókun
  • Analytics and advertising platforms

We do not control these cookies directly.

6) Consent

When you visit our website, you may be presented with a cookie banner requesting your consent to use non-essential cookies.

You can accept or reject cookies at any time.

7) Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
