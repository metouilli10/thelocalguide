BEFORE YOU BOOK

Booking & planning

Prices, reservations, pickup and the details that help you plan.

How much do tours cost in Agadir?

Prices vary by experience and duration. Paradise Valley and quad biking currently start from €25 per person. Day trips and longer activities have different prices. Check each tour page for its current price, inclusions and pickup details.

Can I book Agadir tours online?

Yes. Open the tour page you are interested in and choose “Book now on WhatsApp” to request availability directly. You can also call +212 700-006462 or use the contact page to prepare an email. For a last-minute trip, WhatsApp is usually the quickest way to ask.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept multiple payment methods for your convenience: cash (EUR, MAD, or USD) on the day of the tour, bank transfer for advance bookings, PayPal for online payments, and credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard) for some bookings. For same-day bookings, cash payment is most common. We can also arrange payment via bank transfer for larger groups. All prices are clearly displayed in EUR, and we accept equivalent amounts in local currency (MAD) or USD.

Is hotel pickup included in the tour price?

Hotel pickup is included on many Agadir tours, but pickup areas and arrangements vary by experience. Check the individual tour page and share your hotel or meeting point so we can confirm the details before you book.

What's your cancellation policy?

We offer free cancellation up to 24 hours before your tour start time for a full refund. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the tour start time are non-refundable. For weather-related cancellations (rare in Agadir), we offer a full refund or the option to reschedule. Group tours may have different policies - check your booking confirmation. To cancel, contact us via WhatsApp, phone, or email as soon as possible.

Do I need to book tours in advance?

We recommend booking at least 24-48 hours in advance, especially during peak season (June-September) and for popular tours like Paradise Valley or Marrakech day trips. Same-day bookings are possible via WhatsApp if availability exists. Booking in advance ensures your preferred date and time, allows us to prepare properly, and gives you peace of mind. Last-minute bookings may have limited availability, especially for small-group tours.

What's included in the tour price?

Inclusions vary by tour. Your tour page lists the transport, guide, equipment, meals or extras that are included, along with anything you may need to pay for separately. Ask us if you want to confirm a specific item before booking.

Do you offer private tours?

Yes! We offer private tours for individuals, couples, families, or groups who want a personalized experience. Private tours allow you to customize the itinerary, set your own pace, and have exclusive access to your guide. Prices vary based on group size and tour type. Private tours are ideal for special occasions, photography enthusiasts, or travelers with specific interests. Contact us via WhatsApp or email to discuss private tour options and pricing.

PLAN YOUR DAY

Getting ready

When to go, what to bring and who can join.

What should I wear on a desert tour?

For desert tours, wear comfortable, lightweight clothing that covers your skin to protect from sun and sand. Long pants and long-sleeved shirts are recommended. Closed-toe shoes (sneakers or hiking boots) are essential - no sandals. Bring a wide-brimmed hat, sunglasses, and high SPF sunscreen. In cooler months (November-March), bring layers as desert temperatures can drop significantly. Avoid loose jewelry that could get lost in sand.

What's the best time to visit Agadir?

The best time to visit Agadir is from March to November when the weather is warm and sunny. Spring (March-May) and autumn (September-November) offer the most pleasant temperatures (20-28°C) perfect for outdoor activities. Summer (June-August) is hot (25-35°C) but great for beach activities. Winter (December-February) is mild (15-22°C) and less crowded. Peak tourist season is July-August, so book early. Weather is generally sunny year-round with minimal rainfall.

Do I need travel insurance for Agadir tours?

While not mandatory, we strongly recommend travel insurance for all tours, especially adventure activities like quad biking, sandboarding, or jet skiing. Travel insurance should cover medical expenses, trip cancellation, and adventure activities. Most standard travel insurance policies cover our tours, but check that adventure sports are included. We have basic liability coverage, but your personal travel insurance provides additional protection for medical emergencies, lost belongings, or trip interruptions.

Are Agadir tours family-friendly?

Many of our experiences welcome families, including Paradise Valley, camel rides, city tours and cooking classes. Age limits and child prices vary by activity, especially for quad bikes and other adventure tours. Check the relevant tour page or ask us about your children’s ages before booking.

What languages do tour guides speak?

Our professional tour guides are multilingual and speak English, French, Arabic, and often Spanish or German. All our guides are fluent in English and French, which are the most common languages for international tourists. When booking, you can request a guide who speaks your preferred language. Our guides are certified, knowledgeable about local culture and history, and excellent communicators who make tours engaging and informative.

How far is Paradise Valley from Agadir?

Paradise Valley is approximately 60-70 kilometers (37-43 miles) from Agadir city center. The drive takes about 1.5 hours through scenic mountain roads and Berber villages. Our tours include comfortable air-conditioned transportation with hotel pickup and drop-off. The journey itself is part of the experience, offering stunning views of the Atlas Mountains and traditional Moroccan countryside.

Are the tours suitable for solo travelers?

Absolutely! Our tours are perfect for solo travelers. We operate small-group tours (typically 4-8 people) which creates a friendly, social atmosphere. Solo travelers often find it easy to connect with other travelers. Our guides are welcoming and ensure everyone feels included. If you prefer a private tour, we can arrange that for an additional fee. Many solo travelers book our tours and have wonderful experiences.

What should I bring to Paradise Valley?

For Paradise Valley, bring: comfortable hiking shoes with good grip (essential - no flip-flops), swimsuit and towel (you can change at the pools), sun protection (hat, sunglasses, high SPF sunscreen), lightweight quick-dry clothing, water bottle (at least 1 liter per person), camera or phone for photos, and a small backpack. The hike involves 1.5-2 hours of walking on uneven terrain, so proper footwear is crucial. Swimming in the natural pools is optional but highly recommended!

Can I do multiple tours in one day?

Yes, you can combine tours, but we recommend spacing them out for the best experience. Popular combinations include: morning quad biking + afternoon camel ride, Paradise Valley morning + city tour afternoon, or jet ski + beach time. Some tours like Marrakech or Essaouira day trips are full-day (8-10 hours) and can't be combined. Contact us to create a customized itinerary. We can arrange transportation between tours and ensure timing works smoothly.

Is Paradise Valley suitable for elderly travelers?

Paradise Valley requires moderate fitness and involves 1.5-2 hours of hiking on uneven, rocky terrain with some inclines. It's not recommended for travelers with mobility issues, severe knee/back problems, or difficulty walking on uneven surfaces. However, if you're reasonably fit and mobile, age alone isn't a barrier. The pace is comfortable with frequent stops. For elderly travelers, we recommend our city tours, camel rides, cooking classes, or Essaouira day trip which are less physically demanding.

How long are the tours?

Tour durations vary: Half-day tours (Paradise Valley, Quad Biking, Camel Rides) are typically 3-5 hours including transportation. Full-day trips (Marrakech, Essaouira) are 8-10 hours. Adventure activities (Jet Ski, Sandboarding) are usually 2-4 hours. City tours are 2-3 hours. Exact durations are listed on each tour page. Times include hotel pickup/drop-off. We always provide accurate timing so you can plan your day accordingly.

WHILE YOU EXPLORE

On the experience

Safety, weather and the little things that make the day easy.

Is Agadir safe for tourists?

Yes, Agadir is considered one of the safest cities in Morocco for tourists. The city has a modern infrastructure, low crime rates, and is very welcoming to international visitors. Our tours are conducted by certified local guides who prioritize your safety. We recommend standard travel precautions: keep valuables secure, be aware of your surroundings, and follow your guide's instructions.

What happens if it rains?

Agadir has a very dry climate with minimal rainfall (especially April-October). If light rain occurs, most tours continue as planned. For heavy rain or severe weather, we may modify the itinerary or offer rescheduling. Water activities like jet skiing may be postponed for safety. Desert tours are usually fine in light rain. We monitor weather closely and will contact you if changes are needed. Full refunds are offered if we cancel due to weather.

Are tips expected for guides?

Tips are not mandatory but are greatly appreciated by our guides who work hard to provide excellent experiences. If you're happy with your tour, a tip of 10-20% of the tour price or 5-10 EUR per person is a generous gesture. Tips can be given directly to your guide at the end of the tour. It's entirely optional - our guides are professional regardless. Many travelers tip as a way to show appreciation for exceptional service.

Can I take photos during tours?

Yes! Photography is encouraged on all our tours. The stunning landscapes, cultural sites, and activities provide amazing photo opportunities. Bring your camera, smartphone, or GoPro. Our guides know the best photo spots and are happy to take photos of you. For some activities like quad biking, we provide secure storage for cameras. Drones may have restrictions in certain areas - check with your guide. We love seeing your photos - tag us on social media!
