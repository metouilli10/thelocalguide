## Goal
Turn this page into the main commercial hub for `agadir day trips` and `day trips from agadir`, so it can rank for broad excursion intent and distribute authority to the site's core day-trip money pages.

## Current Issues
- Current title/meta mismatch: the title is descriptive, but it reads like a travel blog list rather than a high-intent booking hub.
- Weak intro or unclear scope: the lead is informative, but it does not immediately frame the page as the place to compare excursions, prices, drive times, and booking options.
- Thin destination coverage: only the first 5 trips have real sections even though the page promises 15 day trips.
- Weak commercial assist: the page links to some key tours, but it does not use clear trip cards, trust signals, or route-based booking pathways.
- Weak internal-link support: Souss Massa and other core linked money pages are missing from the main body even though the research says this hub should push authority into them.
- Weak FAQ coverage: there is no FAQ for best day trips by travel style, how long transfers take, or whether hotel pickup is included.
- Pricing trust issue: sidebar prices and body prices should be validated against the linked tour pages before surfacing snippets more aggressively.
