## Competitor Or Winner Patterns To Reuse
- Broad commercial pages win with price visibility, route density, trust cues, and strong comparison logic.
- Hybrid guide pages win when logistics, seasonality, transport choice, and realistic expectations are easy to scan.
- Money pages win when they answer objections early: total duration, pickup, what is included, and whether the effort is worth it.
