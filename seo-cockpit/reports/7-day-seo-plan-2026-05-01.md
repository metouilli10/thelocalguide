### Day 6: `2026-05-06`

## Content Rules For New Articles
- Every new article should support one money page, not compete with it.
- Each article should include:
  - one clear primary query cluster
  - one comparison or pricing section
  - one FAQ block
  - one main CTA into the matching tour page
  - two to four internal links into related hubs and comparison guides
- Do not publish broad inspiration pieces this week unless they directly support a core revenue cluster.
