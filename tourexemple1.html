Overview

Escape the city and discover the natural beauty of Paradise Valley on this half-day excursion from Agadir. Hidden in the High Atlas Mountains, this oasis offers crystal-clear pools, palm trees, and breathtaking rock formations. It's the perfect getaway for nature lovers and those looking to experience the authentic Moroccan countryside.

Highlights

  • Swim in crystal-clear natural pools surrounded by palm trees.
  • Scenic drive through Atlas foothills and Berber villages.
  • Visit a local argan oil cooperative (optional stop).
  • Small-group experience with a friendly local guide.
  • Perfect for couples, families and friends.
  • Short, easy hike suitable for most fitness levels.

Itinerary

We'll pick you up directly from your hotel reception in Agadir or Taghazout in a comfortable, air-conditioned vehicle.

A short, interesting stop to learn about the traditional production of Argan oil by local Berber women. You might even see goats climbing the Argan trees!

We arrive at the valley entrance. Enjoy a scenic 20-minute hike through palm groves and rock formations to reach the heart of the oasis.

The best part! Swim in the natural rock pools, sunbathe on the warm rocks, or relax in the shade with a refreshing drink from a local cafe.

After a relaxing day, we drive back to Agadir and drop you off at your hotel around 2:30 PM - 3:00 PM.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this tour suitable for children?
Yes, this tour is very family-friendly. The walk to the pools is about 20 minutes on an uneven path, so it's not suitable for strollers, but children who can walk that distance (or be carried) will love the water and nature.
What should I bring?
We recommend bringing comfortable walking shoes (sneakers are fine), swimwear, a towel, sunscreen, sunglasses, and a bottle of water. Don't forget your camera for the beautiful views!
Is lunch included?
Lunch is not included in the standard price to give you flexibility. There are local cafes in the valley where you can purchase a traditional tagine (approx. €10) or drinks. You are also welcome to bring your own snacks.
Can I be picked up from Taghazout?
Yes! We offer free pickup from all hotels in Agadir, Taghazout, Tamraght, and the cruise port. Just let us know your hotel name when booking.
Can I book last minute?
Yes, we accept bookings up to 12 hours before the tour starts, subject to availability. For very last-minute inquiries, please contact us via WhatsApp for instant confirmation.
