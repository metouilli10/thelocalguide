Overview

Want to see Marrakech without changing hotels? This full-day tour is the easiest way to do it. You leave Agadir in the morning, spend the day on guided highlights plus free time in the medina, then return the same evening.

The day is structured for first-time visitors who want the Red City experience without dealing with bus schedules or an overnight stay. For €38 per person, the trip includes hotel pickup, transport, guide support, Bahia Palace entry, and a clear full-day route.

The trade-off is simple: it is a long day, but it gives you Marrakech's biggest highlights without the friction of planning intercity transport yourself. Itinerary

From early pickup in Agadir to evening return, this itinerary is built to cover Marrakech's essential highlights in one well-paced day.

1

Hotel Pickup & Scenic Drive

Start early from Agadir and travel toward Marrakech with a comfort break on the way. The route is long, but the changing scenery makes the journey part of the experience.

3.5 hours drive • Pickup included
2

Koutoubia Mosque & Arrival in Marrakech

Arrive in Marrakech and begin with an orientation stop at Koutoubia Mosque. It's the perfect first landmark before heading deeper into the old city.

30 minutes • Guided visit
3

Bahia Palace Exploration

Explore the ornate courtyards and rooms of Bahia Palace with your guide, then continue into the heart of Marrakech.

1 hour • Entry tickets included
4

Lunch Break

Enjoy free time for lunch at a restaurant of your choice before the afternoon walking tour continues.

1.5 hours • Lunch not included
5

Souks & Artisan Workshops

Follow your guide through the souks, learn how the market is organized, and enjoy time to browse or shop.

2 hours • Free time for shopping
6

Jemaa el-Fnaa Square Experience

Take in the atmosphere of Jemaa el-Fnaa, then pause at a rooftop cafe for panoramic views and traditional mint tea.

1.5 hours • Rooftop tea included
7

Return Journey to Agadir

Depart Marrakech in the evening and return comfortably to your hotel in Agadir after a full day of sightseeing.

3.5 hours • Hotel drop-off included

Meeting Point & Pickup Details

We offer free hotel pickup and drop-off from all accommodations in Agadir, Taghazout, and surrounding areas. This full-day tour requires an early start to maximize your time in Marrakech, so we'll collect you bright and early to begin your adventure.

Pickup Locations

  • All hotels in Agadir city center and beachfront
  • Hotels and riads in Taghazout (15 min north)
  • Accommodations in Aourir and Tamraght
  • Agadir Marina and port area
  • If your accommodation is outside these areas, we'll arrange a convenient meeting point

Pickup Times

  • Morning pickup: Between 7:00-7:30 AM (early start for full day)
  • Exact pickup time confirmed via WhatsApp the evening before
  • Please be ready 5 minutes before your scheduled pickup time
  • Return to hotel: Approximately 9:30-10:00 PM (after sunset)
  • Total tour duration: 12-13 hours including travel time

Important Information

What to Bring

  • Comfortable walking shoes: You will walk a lot in the medina, so wear closed shoes with good support.
  • Light, modest clothing: Choose breathable clothes that cover shoulders and knees for comfort and local customs.
  • Sun protection: Bring sunscreen, sunglasses, and a hat, especially in warmer months.
  • Water and small essentials: Carry a water bottle, tissues, and any personal medication you may need.
  • Cash for extras: Bring some cash for lunch, drinks, tips, and optional shopping in the souks.

Health & Safety

  • Small group tours ensure personalized attention
  • All vehicles are regularly maintained and insured
  • Professional guides trained in first aid
  • Free cancellation up to 24 hours before departure
  • Flexible itinerary adapted to weather conditions

Important Note

  • This is a full-day tour (approximately 12 hours)
  • Moderate walking required - comfortable shoes essential
  • Please inform us of any dietary restrictions in advance
  • Pickup time may vary slightly based on hotel location
  • Marrakech can be hot - dress appropriately and bring water
