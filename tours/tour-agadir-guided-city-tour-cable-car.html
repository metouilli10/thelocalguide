Itinerary

This half-day route keeps the pace simple and covers Agadir's main viewpoints, local market, and waterfront in one easy trip.

1

Hotel Pickup & Welcome

15-20 minutes

Your guide collects you from your hotel in Agadir and gives a short introduction on the way to the first stop. It is an easy start that sets the plan for the morning and gives quick local context before sightseeing begins.

2

Kasbah Agadir Oufella

30-40 minutes

Stop at the Kasbah for the city's best panoramic view and a quick look at Agadir's history. Your guide explains the old fortress, the 1960 earthquake, and how the city was rebuilt while you have time for photos over the bay.

3

Cable Car Ride Experience

45-60 minutes

Ride the cable car for wide views over Agadir Bay, the beach, and the city below. The round-trip ticket is included, and this stop gives you the clearest look at Agadir's coastal layout from above.

4

Souk El Had Market Visit

45-60 minutes

Walk through Souk El Had with your guide for a practical introduction to Agadir's busiest market. You get time to browse spices, crafts, and everyday stalls while learning how the souk is organized and where to shop.

5

Agadir Marina & Return

30-40 minutes

Finish with a short stop at the marina for a look at modern Agadir and an easy walk along the waterfront. After that, your driver takes you back to your hotel to end the tour.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Agadir cable car (téléphérique Agadir) included?

Yes. The Agadir cable car ticket is included in your tour price and covers one round-trip ride. You go up for the panoramic views over the bay and city, then return down as part of the guided half-day route.

Is the cable car safe? What if I'm afraid of heights?

Yes, the Agadir cable car is very safe and regularly maintained. The cabins are enclosed and secure. However, if you're uncomfortable with heights, you can remain at the base station while others in your group enjoy the ride. Our guide will ensure your comfort throughout the experience.

What happens if the cable car is closed due to weather?

In rare cases when the cable car is closed due to high winds or maintenance, we'll provide an alternative itinerary. This may include extended time at the Kasbah, a visit to additional local attractions, or extended market exploration. Full refunds are available if the tour cannot be completed.

How long do we spend at Souk El Had market?

You'll spend approximately 45-60 minutes at Souk El Had market. Your guide will lead you through the main sections, pointing out interesting stalls and helping with any shopping. This gives you enough time to explore, shop, and experience the vibrant atmosphere without feeling rushed.

Can children join this tour?

Yes, children are welcome! The tour is suitable for all ages. Children under 12 may receive discounted cable car tickets (please inquire when booking). The tour involves moderate walking, so strollers for young children are recommended for the market and marina sections.

Is hotel pickup available from all Agadir hotels?

Yes, we provide complimentary pickup from all hotels in Agadir city center and the main hotel zones. Please provide your hotel name and address when booking. For hotels outside the city center, pickup may require an additional fee or we can arrange a meeting point convenient to you.

Pickup and preparation

Pickup Locations

  • All hotels in Agadir city center and beachfront
  • Hotels and riads in Taghazout (15 min north)
  • Accommodations in Aourir and Tamraght
  • Agadir Marina and port area
  • If your accommodation is outside these areas, we'll arrange a convenient meeting point

Pickup Times

  • Morning pickup: Between 9:00-10:00 AM
  • Exact pickup time confirmed via WhatsApp the evening before
  • Please be ready 5 minutes before your scheduled pickup time
  • Return to hotel: Approximately 1:00-2:00 PM
  • Total tour duration: 3-4 hours including travel time

What to Bring

  • Comfortable shoes: You will walk through the Kasbah area, the souk, and the marina, so supportive shoes are the best option.
  • Sun protection: Bring sunglasses, sunscreen, and a hat, especially for the Kasbah and cable car stops where shade is limited.
  • Water: A small bottle of water is useful, particularly on warmer days.
  • Cash: Bring some cash if you want to shop in Souk El Had or leave an optional tip for the guide.
  • Phone or camera: The Kasbah and cable car are the main photo stops, so keep your device charged.
  • Light layer: A thin jacket can help if the morning starts cool or the Kasbah feels breezy.

Health & Safety

  • This is an easy sightseeing tour, but there is some walking and standing at each stop.
  • Guests should be comfortable riding the cable car and being at height for a short period.
  • The tour runs in normal weather, but the cable car can pause in strong wind or for operational reasons.
  • Souk El Had can get busy, so keep personal belongings secure in crowded areas.

Before You Book

  • Please send your hotel or meeting-point details after booking so pickup can be arranged correctly.
  • Souk El Had is closed on Mondays, so the market stop may be adjusted if needed.
  • If the cable car is unavailable due to weather or operations, the guide will adapt the route on the day.
  • Free cancellation is available up to 24 hours before the tour start time.
