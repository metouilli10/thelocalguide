Overview

This page is positioned for the core camel ride Agadir query rather than only the Tamri place name. The experience is designed as an easy, scenic evening activity: hotel pickup, a relaxed beach ride at sunset, and dinner after the ride instead of a rushed turnaround.

Itinerary

The evening is paced simply so you can enjoy the ride without feeling rushed.

1

Hotel Pickup & Beach Arrival

Pickup is usually scheduled in the late afternoon. Once you arrive, the guide introduces the camels, explains how to mount comfortably, and gets the group ready for the ride.

30 minutes • Transportation included
2

Sunset Camel Ride

The ride follows a gentle beach route at walking pace, making it suitable for first-timers. Sunset is the highlight here: cooler air, softer light, and a quieter atmosphere for photos.

45-60 minutes • Professional guide included
3

Dinner After the Ride

Once the ride finishes, you settle in for dinner and have time to unwind. This part of the experience is more about relaxing, eating, and enjoying the atmosphere before heading back.

60-75 minutes • Dinner included
4

Return to Your Hotel

After dinner, the driver takes you back to your hotel. Exact return time depends on sunset and pickup area, and is confirmed when your booking is scheduled.

30 minutes • Tea & return transfer included

Meeting Point & Pickup Details

Pickup is arranged around sunset and confirmed by WhatsApp. Times change during the year depending on the season and your location.

Pickup Locations

  • Hotels in Agadir city center and beach area
  • Selected pickups from Taghazout, Aourir, and Tamraght
  • Alternative meeting point if your stay is outside the normal route

Pickup Times

  • Pickup time depends on sunset and season
  • Confirmation is sent the day before via WhatsApp
  • Return is usually later the same evening after dinner

Important Information

What to Bring

  • Comfortable clothes you can sit and ride in
  • Long pants and closed shoes are recommended
  • A light layer for the evening breeze
  • Your phone or camera for sunset photos
  • Cash for optional extras or tips

Health & Safety

  • Suitable for beginners and casual riders
  • Brief riding instructions are given before departure
  • Not recommended for pregnant travelers or severe back issues
  • Please tell us in advance if you have mobility concerns

Before You Book

  • Pickup time changes through the year to match sunset
  • Please share dietary requests in advance
  • Young children may need to ride with an adult
  • If weather is poor, timing may be adjusted
