Frequently asked questions

Do I need previous buggy or off-road driving experience?

No. The activity starts with a briefing and short practice so beginners can get comfortable before heading onto the route.

What safety measures are in place?

You receive a mandatory safety briefing and equipment before the ride. The guide leads the route and keeps the group at a controlled pace.

What should I wear for the buggy tour?

Wear comfortable clothes that can get dusty, plus closed shoes. Sunglasses, sunscreen, and a light layer are also a good idea.

Can I bring a camera or phone on the buggy?

Yes, but keep devices secure while driving. Use the scheduled stops for photos rather than filming while moving.

What happens if the weather is bad?

If conditions are unsafe, the operator may delay, adjust, or reschedule the route. You will be contacted in advance if there is a change.

A little more about the day

This buggy tour is built for travelers who want a stronger off-road feel than a standard sightseeing trip. After pickup, you get a safety briefing and a short introduction to the buggy before heading out on a guided route outside Agadir.

The focus is on driving, scenery, and simple logistics. The terrain changes through the ride, but the pace stays manageable for beginners and casual drivers.
