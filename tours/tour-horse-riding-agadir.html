Itinerary

The itinerary is simple and easy to follow: pickup, horse matching, a short briefing, then a guided ride before your return transfer.

1

Hotel Pickup from Agadir

15-20 minutes

We collect you from your hotel or agreed meeting point and drive you to the ranch. Pickup time is confirmed after booking and depends on the departure you choose. The transfer is short and gives you time to ask questions before the ride starts.

2

Arrive at Equestrian Center

15 minutes

At the ranch, the team introduces you to the horses and matches you with one that suits your size and riding level. This is where beginners can mention any concerns and experienced riders can request a more active pace if conditions allow.

3

Safety Briefing & Equipment

10-15 minutes

Before departure, your guide explains how to mount, hold the reins, and follow the route safely. Helmets are provided, and first-time riders get a short introduction so they feel comfortable before heading out.

4

Beach Horseback Riding

45-60 minutes

The main part of the tour is a guided coastal ride with open views, fresh sea air, and several good photo spots. The pace usually starts gently, then may increase slightly depending on the group's confidence and the guide's judgment.

5

Explore Berber Villages

30-40 minutes

Some departures may include a short inland section on quieter tracks near the ranch before looping back. The exact route depends on the tide, weather, and riding conditions on the day.

6

Tea Stop at Ranch Tamri

Tea included; BBQ dinner with the €55 adult option

After the ride, relax with tea at Ranch Tamri. If you choose the BBQ dinner option, stay at the ranch for dinner before your return transfer.

7

Sunset Viewing & Return (Sunset Tour)

30 minutes

If you book a later departure, you may catch softer evening light and sunset views during the ride. After tea or your optional BBQ dinner, we drive you back to your hotel or meeting point.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need previous horse riding experience?

No. This ride is beginner-friendly, and the team matches each guest with a suitable horse. First-time riders receive a short briefing before departure, while more experienced riders can usually enjoy a slightly more active pace if conditions allow.

What should I wear for the horse riding tour?

Wear long pants and closed shoes such as sneakers or light boots. Bring sunscreen, water, and a light layer for later departures. Helmets are provided on site.

How long is the horse riding tour?

The ride is approximately 1.5 hours. Allow around 2 to 3 hours for the standard experience including transfers and the tea stop. The BBQ dinner option takes longer.

Is horse riding in Agadir good for beginners?

Yes. This is one of the easier horse riding options in Agadir for beginners because the pace is guided, the horses are selected to suit mixed experience levels, and handlers explain the basics before the ride starts.

Is the horse riding tour suitable for children?

Yes, in most cases. The standard ride is €25 per child. Children should be comfortable around animals and accompanied by an adult. Let us know their age before booking so we can confirm suitability and prepare the right horse. Ask us about a child's BBQ dinner price.

What happens if the weather is bad?

If weather, tide, or beach conditions are not suitable, we may adjust the route, move the departure time, or reschedule the activity. If the tour cannot operate safely, we will contact you with the available options.

Can I take photos during the horse riding tour?

Yes. There are photo opportunities during the ride, and guides often help guests take pictures during the stops. Keep your phone or camera secure while riding.

What is the difference between the morning and sunset tours?

The route is similar, but the atmosphere changes. Daytime departures usually offer brighter light and clearer visibility, while later departures can feel cooler and may include softer sunset colors.

A little more about the day

Enjoy horse riding in Agadir with hotel pickup, a guided coastal ride, and calm horses for beginners. We take you to Ranch Tamri on Banana Beach, match you with a suitable horse, and guide you on a relaxed ride along the coast before stopping for tea at the ranch.

The ride lasts approximately 1.5 hours. Allow around 2 to 3 hours for the standard experience including transfers and the tea stop. The ride costs €45 per adult or €25 per child. Choose the ride with BBQ dinner for €55 per adult; allow extra time for dinner.

Before booking, the key details are straightforward: small groups, pickup included, and a route suited to relaxed sightseeing rather than advanced riding. If you are comparing beach activities, check the sunset camel ride Agadir page for a more traditional option or quad biking Agadir if you want more speed and sand.

Pickup and preparation

Pickup Locations

  • All hotels in Agadir city center and beachfront
  • Hotels and riads in Taghazout (15 min north)
  • Accommodations in Aourir and Tamraght
  • Agadir Marina and port area
  • If your accommodation is outside these areas, we'll arrange a convenient meeting point

Pickup Times

  • Start times vary by season and selected departure
  • Exact pickup time is confirmed after booking
  • Please be ready 5 to 10 minutes before pickup
  • Total experience is usually around 2 to 3 hours door to door
  • Later departures are best if you want softer sunset light

What to Bring

  • Comfortable long pants: Help prevent rubbing and keep you comfortable in the saddle.
  • Closed-toe shoes: Wear sneakers or light boots with good grip. Avoid sandals and flip-flops.
  • Sun essentials: Bring sunscreen, sunglasses, and a cap for daytime departures.
  • Phone or camera: You will have great photo moments along the route.
  • Water and a light layer: Stay hydrated and bring a light jacket for breezy or late rides.

Health & Safety

  • Wearing the provided helmet is mandatory for all riders
  • Maximum rider weight is 95 kg (210 lbs)
  • Not suitable for pregnant travelers
  • Children should ride with adult supervision
  • Basic mobility is required for mounting and dismounting
  • The horses are used to guided tourist rides
  • Follow the guide's instructions throughout the activity

Before You Book

  • Tell us in advance if you have limited riding experience or mobility concerns
  • The route can change depending on tide, weather, and beach conditions
  • Sunset views depend on the season and daily visibility
  • Your booking is confirmed once availability is checked
  • Free cancellation is available up to 24 hours before departure
