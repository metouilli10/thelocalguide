Overview

If you want an easy outdoor activity that feels special without taking up your whole day, this is one of the best-value rides in Agadir. We pick you up from your hotel, take you to the ranch, match you with a suitable horse, and guide you on a relaxed ride near the beach. It is a strong choice for first-time riders, couples, and families looking for something calm, photogenic, and easy to fit into the day.

The full experience takes around 2 to 3 hours including transfers, with roughly 1.5 hours of riding time. You will follow a guide at a comfortable pace, with time for photos and sea views along the route. Depending on the departure you choose, you can ride in bright daytime light or plan your outing later for softer sunset colors.

Before booking, the key details are straightforward: small groups, pickup included, and a route suited to relaxed sightseeing rather than advanced riding. Our handlers work with both beginners and experienced riders, and the overall experience is designed to feel easy, safe, and memorable.

Itinerary

The itinerary is simple and easy to follow: pickup, horse matching, a short briefing, then a guided ride before your return transfer.

1

Hotel Pickup from Agadir

We collect you from your hotel or agreed meeting point and drive you to the ranch. Pickup time is confirmed after booking and depends on the departure you choose. The transfer is short and gives you time to ask questions before the ride starts.

15-20 minutes • Free hotel transfer included
2

Arrive at Equestrian Center

At the ranch, the team introduces you to the horses and matches you with one that suits your size and riding level. This is where beginners can mention any concerns and experienced riders can request a more active pace if conditions allow.

15 minutes • Horse assignment & introduction
3

Safety Briefing & Equipment

Before departure, your guide explains how to mount, hold the reins, and follow the route safely. Helmets are provided, and first-time riders get a short introduction so they feel comfortable before heading out.

10-15 minutes • Safety equipment provided
4

Beach Horseback Riding

The main part of the tour is a guided coastal ride with open views, fresh sea air, and several good photo spots. The pace usually starts gently, then may increase slightly depending on the group's confidence and the guide's judgment.

45-60 minutes • Photo stops included
5

Explore Berber Villages

Some departures may include a short inland section on quieter tracks near the ranch before looping back. The exact route depends on the tide, weather, and riding conditions on the day.

30-40 minutes • Cultural insights & village visit
6

Sunset Viewing & Return (Sunset Tour)

If you book a later departure, you may catch softer evening light and sunset views during the ride. After the route ends, we return you to the ranch and drive you back to your hotel or meeting point.

30 minutes • Return transfer included

Meeting Point & Pickup Details

Pickup is included from the main tourist areas around Agadir. If you are staying outside the standard zone, we can usually arrange a nearby meeting point.

Pickup Locations

  • All hotels in Agadir city center and beachfront
  • Hotels and riads in Taghazout (15 min north)
  • Accommodations in Aourir and Tamraght
  • Agadir Marina and port area
  • If your accommodation is outside these areas, we'll arrange a convenient meeting point

Pickup Times

  • Start times vary by season and selected departure
  • Exact pickup time is confirmed after booking
  • Please be ready 5 to 10 minutes before pickup
  • Total experience is usually around 2 to 3 hours door to door
  • Later departures are best if you want softer sunset light

Important Information

What to Bring

  • Comfortable long pants: Help prevent rubbing and keep you comfortable in the saddle.
  • Closed-toe shoes: Wear sneakers or light boots with good grip. Avoid sandals and flip-flops.
  • Sun essentials: Bring sunscreen, sunglasses, and a cap for daytime departures.
  • Phone or camera: You will have great photo moments along the route.
  • Water and a light layer: Stay hydrated and bring a light jacket for breezy or late rides.

Health & Safety

  • Wearing the provided helmet is mandatory for all riders
  • Maximum rider weight is 95 kg (210 lbs)
  • Not suitable for pregnant travelers
  • Children should ride with adult supervision
  • Basic mobility is required for mounting and dismounting
  • The horses are used to guided tourist rides
  • Follow the guide's instructions throughout the activity

Before You Book

  • Tell us in advance if you have limited riding experience or mobility concerns
  • The route can change depending on tide, weather, and beach conditions
  • Sunset views depend on the season and daily visibility
  • Your booking is confirmed once availability is checked
  • Free cancellation is available up to 24 hours before departure
