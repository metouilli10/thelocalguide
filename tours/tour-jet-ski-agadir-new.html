Itinerary

This is a simple, fast-paced water activity: check in at the marina, get a short safety briefing, ride with a guide, and return after your session.

1

Arrival & Safety Briefing

15 minutes

Meet the team at Agadir Marina, check in, and get fitted with your life jacket. Before you go out, the guide will explain the controls, riding limits, and the basic safety rules so first-time riders can start with confidence.

2

Jet Ski Adventure on Atlantic Waters

30 min - 1 hour

Head out from the marina and ride along the coast with your guide leading the route. Depending on the session you book, you will have around 30 to 60 minutes on the water, with enough time to enjoy the speed, sea spray, and open views without turning it into a long excursion.

3

Return to Marina & Debrief

15 minutes

Return to the marina, dock with help from the guide, and take a few final photos before changing. The whole experience stays compact, which makes it easy to fit into a beach day or combine with another activity in Agadir.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a license to ride a jet ski in Agadir?

No. You receive a safety briefing and basic operating instructions before the session, so beginners can join without a license.

How long does the jet ski tour last?

You can usually choose between a 30-minute or 60-minute ride. Total time on site is a bit longer because it also includes check-in and the safety briefing.

Is jet skiing safe? What safety measures are in place?

Yes, when conditions are suitable. Life jackets are provided, the guide explains the controls before departure, and the ride is supervised in designated areas.

Can two people ride on one jet ski?

Yes, many jet skis can take two people. Final approval depends on the operator's limits for weight, balance, and sea conditions on the day.

How much does jet ski in Agadir cost?

The starting price on this page is €60 for the shorter session. Longer ride options can cost more, so confirm the exact duration and live price when booking.

Is this jet ski rental or a guided session?

This page is positioned as a guided marina session, not an unsupported free-ride rental. You get a briefing, safety equipment, and guide supervision on the water.

What happens if the weather is bad?

If the sea is too rough or conditions are unsafe, the session may be delayed or rescheduled. If a suitable alternative is not possible, you should be offered another date or a refund according to the booking terms.

What should I wear for jet skiing in Agadir?

Wear swimwear or quick-dry clothing, and bring a towel plus a change of clothes. Avoid loose items that can fall into the water.

A little more about the day
