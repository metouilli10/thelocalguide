Itinerary

Your morning camel ride begins with pickup from Agadir and continues to the Timlaline dunes for a guided ride and a mint tea stop.

1

Hotel Pickup

15-30 minutes

We'll pick you up from your hotel in Agadir between 8:00 AM and 9:00 AM and travel to the Timlaline area. The morning timing helps avoid the midday heat.

2

Timlaline Arrival & Camel Preparation

15 minutes

On arrival in Timlaline, meet your camel guides and the camels. You'll receive a brief safety orientation and help mounting before the guided ride across the sandy terrain.

3

Morning Camel Ride

45-60 minutes

Enjoy a peaceful 45-60 minute guided camel ride through the Timlaline dunes. Take in the sandy landscape and pause for photos along the route.

4

Traditional Moroccan Mint Tea

20-30 minutes

After your camel ride, relax and enjoy a traditional Moroccan mint tea ceremony. This authentic experience allows you to learn about Moroccan tea culture while sipping the refreshing beverage. It's the perfect way to conclude your morning adventure and reflect on your memorable experience.

5

Return to Hotel

15-30 minutes

After the tea ceremony, we'll drive you back to your hotel in Agadir. You'll arrive refreshed and with wonderful memories of your morning camel ride experience. The entire tour typically concludes around 11:00 AM, leaving you with plenty of time to enjoy the rest of your day.

Frequently asked questions

Is the morning camel ride suitable for children?

Yes. This is one of the easier activities to do with children because the camels move slowly and the morning slot is usually more comfortable.

What should I wear for the camel ride?

Wear comfortable clothing, with long pants and closed shoes if possible. A light layer is useful early in the morning.

How long is the actual camel ride?

The ride itself is usually around 45 to 60 minutes. The total booking time is longer because it also includes pickup and return.

Can I take photos during the ride?

Yes. Morning light is usually good for photos, and the guide can help at the stops.

What happens if it rains?

If conditions are poor, the operator may reschedule or adjust the departure time. You will be contacted in advance if needed.

Is there a weight limit for the camel ride?

Weight limits can apply for rider comfort and animal welfare. If you are unsure, mention it when booking so the team can confirm.

A little more about the day

Explore the Timlaline dunes on a gentle morning camel ride after pickup from Agadir. The guided route moves at an easy pace, with time to enjoy the sandy landscape and take photos.

It is a strong fit for families, couples, and first-time riders who want something calm, simple, and low effort. The experience focuses on the ride itself, with guide support from pickup to return.

Pickup and preparation

What to Bring: Comfortable clothing suitable for riding; Sunscreen and hat for sun protection; Camera or smartphone for photos; Closed-toe shoes (sneakers recommended); Light jacket or sweater (mornings can be cool); Water bottle (optional).

Health & Safety: All camels are well-trained and gentle, suitable for all ages; Safety briefing provided before the ride; Experienced handlers accompany the group at all times; Maximum weight limit: 100 kg (220 lbs) per person; Not recommended for pregnant women or those with back problems; Children under 5 can ride with an adult (shared camel).

Important Note: Tour operates daily, weather permitting; Morning departure times vary by season (typically 8:00-9:00 AM); Please arrive at hotel lobby 10 minutes before pickup time; Tour duration is approximately 2-3 hours including transportation; Small group sizes ensure personalized attention; Free cancellation up to 24 hours before tour start.
