Itinerary

The session follows the classic hammam flow: steam, exfoliation, then massage. It is designed to feel restorative without being overly complicated.

1

Steam Room Relaxation

15-20 minutes

You start in the steam room to warm the body and prepare the skin. It is the gentlest part of the treatment and helps you settle in before the scrub.

2

Black Soap Exfoliation

20-25 minutes

Next comes the black soap and kessa glove exfoliation. This is the most traditional step, and while it can feel firm, it leaves the skin much smoother.

3

Relaxing Massage

45-60 minutes

The session finishes with an argan oil massage. It slows the pace down and gives the treatment a more complete, restorative finish.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a hammam in Agadir cost?

The starting price on this page is €45 per person for the hammam and massage experience. Private requests or extra spa treatments can cost more, so confirm the live price when booking.

What should I wear during the hammam?

You should wear swimwear or underwear during the hammam experience. Towels and bathrobes are provided. The facilities have separate areas for men and women to ensure your comfort and privacy.

Is the hammam experience suitable for everyone?

The hammam is generally safe for most people, but it's not recommended for pregnant women, people with heart conditions, high blood pressure, or those who are claustrophobic. The steam room can be intense, so please inform us of any medical conditions before booking.

How long does the complete experience take?

The complete hammam and massage experience usually takes 2-3 hours, including steam, exfoliation, massage, changing time, and transfer logistics.

Is this a traditional hammam or just a spa treatment?

This experience is built around the classic Moroccan hammam sequence: steam, black soap exfoliation, then massage. It is delivered in a cleaner, visitor-friendly format, but the aim is still a proper traditional hammam in Agadir rather than a basic massage-only spa booking.

Can I book a private session?

Yes, private sessions can usually be arranged on request. If privacy matters to you, mention it when booking on WhatsApp so availability can be confirmed before you arrive.

What products are used during the treatment?

We use authentic Moroccan products including savon beldi (black soap), kessa exfoliating gloves, and argan oil for the massage. That mix is a big part of what makes the treatment feel like a proper hammam rather than a standard spa appointment.

A little more about the day

This is the right hammam in Agadir for travelers who want a proper Moroccan treatment rather than a generic hotel spa. The experience follows the classic flow: steam, black soap exfoliation, and an argan oil massage that helps the session finish slower than it starts.

At €45 per person with hotel pickup and drop-off included, it works especially well after a busier activity day in Agadir. If you are comparing plans, pair it against our jet ski Agadir page for a short adrenaline activity or the Agadir boat trip for a slower marina half-day before you book.

It is also a better fit than a generic spa if you specifically want the classic Moroccan bath sequence rather than just a massage slot. That matters if you are searching for hammam Agadir and want the scrub, steam, and traditional products to be the core of the experience.

Pickup and preparation

What to Bring

  • Swimwear: Swimwear or underwear (required for hammam) - you'll need to wear something during the treatment. Most guests prefer swimwear for comfort. The facilities are private, but you'll be wearing your swimwear during the steam and exfoliation.
  • Change of clothes: Change of clothes for after the treatment - you'll want clean, comfortable clothes to change into after your treatment. Light, comfortable clothing works best.
  • Hydration: Water bottle to stay hydrated - the steam room can be dehydrating, so it's important to drink water. The spa provides water, but bringing your own is convenient.
  • Personal items: Any personal toiletries you prefer - the spa provides towels, robes, and basic toiletries, but you're welcome to bring your own if you have preferences. A hair tie is useful for keeping hair out of the way during treatment.
  • Additional items: Cash for optional tips, and a sense of relaxation - come ready to unwind and enjoy the experience!

Health & Safety

  • Not recommended for pregnant women or people with heart conditions
  • Please inform us of any skin conditions or allergies before treatment
  • Facilities are cleaned and sanitized between each session
  • Professional therapists follow strict hygiene protocols

Before You Book

  • Arrive 15 minutes early for check-in and preparation
  • Separate facilities available for men and women
  • Private sessions can be arranged upon request
  • This is a traditional experience - expect vigorous exfoliation
