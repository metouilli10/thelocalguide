Frequently asked questions

Is quad biking in Agadir beginner-friendly?

Yes. This half-day Agadir quad biking tour is beginner-friendly because the ATVs are automatic, the guide gives a full safety briefing, and the route starts at an easy pace before moving onto desert trails and open tracks.

Do I need a driving license for quad biking in Agadir?

No driving license is needed for this Agadir quad biking tour. The ride takes place off public roads, and the team provides basic training before you start.

Is hotel pickup included?

Yes. Hotel pickup and drop-off from Agadir are included in the tour price, and your exact pickup time is confirmed after booking.

What should I wear for the quad biking tour?

Wear long pants, closed shoes, and comfortable clothes you do not mind getting dusty. Sunglasses, sunscreen, and a scarf or buff for extra dust protection are also a good idea.

How long is the quad biking tour?

The experience follows a half-day format of about 3 hours including hotel transfers, the safety briefing, and guided riding time.
