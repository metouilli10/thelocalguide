Itinerary

The afternoon is split between transfer time, practice, active dune runs, dinner, and the return drive.

1

Hotel Pickup & Desert Transfer

30-45 minutes

Pickup is arranged in the afternoon so you reach the dunes before the best light. The transfer is part of the outing and usually takes under an hour.

2

Safety Briefing & Instruction

20-30 minutes

At the dunes, the guide explains the basics, sets up the boards, and helps everyone start on easy runs before moving on.

3

Sandboarding Adventure & Multiple Runs

2-2.5 hours

This is the active part of the tour. You make several runs, climb back up between descents, and can keep repeating while the group is at the dunes.

4

Sunset & Traditional Moroccan BBQ Dinner

60-75 minutes

Once the main session ends, the group slows down for sunset and dinner. This gives the activity a more complete finish before heading back.

5

Return Journey to Agadir

30-45 minutes

After dinner, you return to Agadir by road. Arrival time depends on the season and the pickup zone.

Frequently asked questions

Is sandboarding safe for beginners?

Yes. Most people join this without previous experience, and the session normally starts on easier dunes first.

What should I wear for sandboarding in Agadir?

Wear comfortable clothes that can get sandy, plus closed shoes. Sunglasses and sunscreen are important in the dunes.

A little more about the day

This is one of the better desert activities for travelers who want something active but still manageable as a first try. After pickup, you head to the dunes, get a short introduction, and start with easier runs before moving to bigger slopes if you want more speed.

The experience is less about technical sport and more about fun, repeated runs, and the desert setting. Sunset timing and dinner afterward make it feel like a full outing rather than just a quick activity.

Pickup and preparation

Pickup Locations

  • All hotels in Agadir city center and beachfront
  • Hotels and riads in Taghazout (15 min north)
  • Accommodations in Aourir and Tamraght
  • Agadir Marina and port area
  • If your accommodation is outside these areas, we'll arrange a convenient meeting point

Pickup Times

  • Summer (June-August): Pickup around 3:00-3:30 PM
  • Spring/Autumn (March-May, Sept-Nov): Pickup around 2:00-2:30 PM
  • Winter (Dec-Feb): Pickup around 1:30-2:00 PM
  • Exact pickup time confirmed via WhatsApp the day before
  • Return to hotel: Approximately 9:00-10:00 PM

What to Bring

  • Closed shoes that are easy to walk in on sand
  • Sunglasses, sunscreen, and a hat
  • Light clothes you do not mind getting sandy
  • A light layer for the evening
  • Your phone or camera for photos

Health & Safety

  • All participants must follow guide instructions for safety
  • Not recommended for pregnant women or those with back/neck problems
  • Children under 8 years must be accompanied by an adult
  • Basic fitness level required - involves climbing sand dunes
  • All safety equipment provided and must be worn during activities

Before You Book

  • Pickup time depends on season and sunset
  • Dune conditions can change the exact session setup
  • Vegetarian requests should be shared before the tour
  • You will walk uphill on sand between runs
