Itinerary

This 2-hour surf lesson takes you from complete beginner to catching your first waves! Starting with hotel pickup, you'll travel to Taghazout Beach where you'll receive professional instruction, all equipment, and hands-on guidance in the water. Perfect for first-timers, this experience will give you the skills and confidence to continue your surfing journey.

1

Hotel Pickup & Arrival at Surf School

15 minutes

You are picked up and taken to the beach in Taghazout, where the instructor helps with the wetsuit, board, and a quick setup before the session starts.

2

In-Water Surfing Practice & Wave Catching

1 hour 15 minutes

Most of the lesson is spent in the water practicing the basics with the instructor close by. The focus is on balance, timing, and getting comfortable on small beginner-friendly waves.

3

Return to the Hotel

15 minutes

After the lesson, you return the equipment, change, and head back. The whole experience stays compact, so it fits easily into the rest of your day.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a 2-hour surf lesson in Agadir cost?

The listed price on this page is €45 per person. It includes the lesson, equipment, and the standard setup described on the page.

Do I need any experience to take a surf lesson?

No. This session is aimed at beginners, and the instructor adapts the lesson to first-timers.

What should I bring to my surf lesson?

Bring swimwear, a towel, sunscreen, water, and dry clothes for after the session.

Can children take surf lessons?

Often yes for older children who can swim and feel comfortable in the water, but it is best to confirm before booking.

What time do surf lessons start?

Sessions usually run in morning and afternoon slots. Your exact pickup time is confirmed after booking.

A little more about the day

This is a simple beginner surf session for travelers who want to try surfing without committing to a full camp or multi-day package. The setup is straightforward: pickup, equipment, instruction, and time in the water.

It works best for first-timers because the lesson stays focused on the basics in a beginner-friendly area of Taghazout. If you want one easy, active experience near Agadir, this is a solid entry point.

Pickup and preparation

We offer free hotel pickup and drop-off from all accommodations in Agadir, Taghazout, and surrounding areas. Our surf lessons take place at Taghazout Beach, one of Morocco's best surf spots, located just 20 minutes north of Agadir.

What to Bring: Swimwear: Swimsuit or bikini (wear under wetsuit) - you'll be wearing this under your wetsuit, so choose something comfortable that won't bunch up. A one-piece or secure two-piece works best. Avoid loose-fitting swimwear that could shift during surfing.; Towels: Towel for drying off after the lesson - you'll want to dry off and change into dry clothes. A quick-dry towel is ideal if you have one.; Sun protection: Sunscreen (SPF 50+ waterproof recommended) - essential for protection from sun and water reflection. Apply before the lesson and bring it for reapplication. A zinc-based sunscreen is ideal for water sports.; Change of clothes: Change of clothes for after the lesson - you'll want comfortable, dry clothes for the ride home. Light, comfortable clothing works best.; Hydration: Water bottle to stay hydrated - surfing is physical activity, and you'll be in the sun, so staying hydrated is important.; Additional items: Camera or waterproof phone case (optional) for additional photos, cash for optional tips, any personal medications, and a sense of adventure!.

Health & Safety: Suitable for ages 8+ (children must be strong swimmers); Basic swimming ability required; Moderate fitness level recommended; Not suitable for pregnant women or those with serious medical conditions; All participants must sign a waiver.

Before You Book: Free cancellation 24 hours before departure; Lessons run daily at 9:00 AM and 2:00 PM; Duration: 2 hours (including briefing and water time); Small groups: Maximum 6 students per instructor; All skill levels welcome - perfect for complete beginners.
