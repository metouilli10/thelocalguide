Frequently asked questions

Is sandboarding suitable for beginners?

Yes. Sandboards and beginner-friendly help are included in both packages.

Do I need to add a quad, buggy or camel ride?

No. The bus transfer from Ranch Tamri to Timlaline and back is included. Optional rides cost extra and are confirmed when booking.

What is included in the morning tour?

Pickup and return in Agadir, Tamraght or Taghazout; stops in Taghazout and Aghroud; sandboarding, canyon viewpoints, mint tea and Moroccan sweets. The morning tour runs 09:00–13:30.

What is included in the sunset tour?

The same route and activities, plus sunset at Timlaline and BBQ dinner at Ranch Tamri. The typical schedule is 15:00–20:00, adjusted seasonally.

How much do children pay?

Children under 12 pay €20 for the morning tour or €35 for the sunset tour.

When is my exact sunset pickup time confirmed?

We confirm seasonal pickup and return times by WhatsApp before booking.

A little more about the day

This Timlaline tour offers pickup in Agadir, Tamraght or Taghazout and brings together three different sides of the coast. After pickup, take a 25–30 minute look around Taghazout, stop at Aghroud's colorful houses and beach, then continue to Ranch Tamri.

At the Timlaline dunes, try sandboarding with boards and beginner help included. See the canyon viewpoints and take photos before returning to the ranch for Moroccan mint tea and sweets. Choose the sunset departure to add sunset at the dunes and BBQ dinner at Ranch Tamri.

Pickup and preparation

We offer pickup and return in Agadir, Tamraght and Taghazout. Share your hotel or pickup location when you inquire; we will confirm the exact meeting point by WhatsApp.

What to Bring: Comfortable shoes for sand and walking; Sun protection and drinking water; Phone or camera for village and canyon photos; A light layer for the sunset tour.

Optional Rides: Quad, buggy and camel rides are round trips between Ranch Tamri and Timlaline; Prices and availability are confirmed when booking; Rider eligibility is confirmed before booking; The included bus remains available if you do not add a ride.

Before You Book: Children under 12 receive the child price; BBQ dinner is included only in the sunset package; Tell us about any mobility or dietary needs when booking; Sunset tour timing changes seasonally.
