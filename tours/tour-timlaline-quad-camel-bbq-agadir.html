Frequently asked questions

How long are the quad and camel rides?

Each guided activity lasts about one hour.

Where is the BBQ served?

The rides take place in the Timlaline area. BBQ dinner is served afterward at Ranch Tamri.

Is hotel pickup included?

Yes, pickup and return from Agadir or Taghazout are included. We confirm your exact pickup point and time by WhatsApp.

Can beginners join?

Yes. The quad ride includes a safety briefing and helmet, and guides lead both activities.

Can children join?

Children are €30 on this promotion. Ask us when booking to confirm participation and rider eligibility.

What time does the tour finish?

The typical return is around 22:00. We confirm the timing for your date when booking.

A little more about the day

Spend an evening in Timlaline with two different ways to explore the dunes. After pickup from Agadir or Taghazout, meet your guides for a safety briefing, then enjoy a guided quad ride and a slower camel trek, each lasting about an hour.

After the rides, return to Ranch Tamri for a traditional Moroccan BBQ, tea or water, and Berber music. The experience is typically 16:00–22:00; your exact pickup and return times are confirmed when booking.
