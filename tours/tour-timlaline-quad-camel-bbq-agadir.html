Timlaline Quad & Camel Ride with BBQ

Two guided rides through the Timlaline dunes, followed by a Moroccan BBQ evening at Ranch Tamri.

Overview

Spend an evening in Timlaline with two different ways to explore the dunes. After pickup from Agadir or Taghazout, meet your guides for a safety briefing, then enjoy a guided quad ride and a slower camel trek, each lasting about an hour.

After the rides, return to Ranch Tamri for a traditional Moroccan BBQ, tea or water, and Berber music. The experience is typically 16:00–22:00; your exact pickup and return times are confirmed when booking.
